Game setup

Shapes

Tick the shapes kids may be asked to do. Each round deals a random set from the ticked shapes. Your own shapes show their name instead of a drawing.

Cards each round

Points

Every card draws its points at random from the ticked values, so a shape that was worth 2 one round can be worth 0 the next.

Card backs

Sounds

Timer

Seconds
Sound when time is up
Music while counting down

During the game, press the space bar to start or stop the timer.

Each round shows a new set of shapes. Ask a question, have the kids make the shape they want and hold it, then flip the cards to see what each shape was worth.
